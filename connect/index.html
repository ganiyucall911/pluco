Account

Create an account, or log in.

PLUCO has no email and no password. Your wallet address is the account: connecting creates it the first time, and opens it every time after. Nothing is signed, and nothing moves.

Create your account.

Your wallet is the account, or use an email and a 6-digit code. No password, no form.

Create with a wallet

Everything runs on Base mainnet. MetaMask or WalletConnect opens your wallet — by extension, QR or the wallet app. With email, a 6-digit code is sent to your inbox and you type it in the same window.
