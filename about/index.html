About

An open market for art on Base.

PLUCO is a registry and a marketplace, not a mint page. Art is the first thing the network carries; the protocol is what stays.

01

Open by default

Anyone can register a collection, mint a piece or buy one. No application, no allowlist, no account. The registry has no owner gate on who may enter.

02

Ownership is explicit

Sellers keep the token until it sells. Royalties read from EIP-2981. Every fee is stated before you sign, not discovered afterwards.

03

Built to be read

The contracts are small, documented and verifiable on Basescan. If you cannot inspect it, you should not trust it.

Status

Contracts written. Deployment next.

The registry, marketplace and reference collection are compiled and tested on a local chain. What remains is a deploy to Base and a wallet to sign it.
