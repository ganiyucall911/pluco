Architecture

Four contracts. No gatekeeper.

01

Registry

The open index. Any ERC-721 on Base can register: artist address, category and metadata. The registry holds no art and takes no fee. Delisting is the artist's choice, not a gatekeeper's.

02

Collections

PlucoOriginals is the reference collection: one-of-one tokens, immutable supply, provenance hash and an owner reserve. Any artist can deploy their own or bring an existing contract.

03

Marketplace

Non-custodial settlement. Sellers keep the token and approve the contract. Buyers pay exactly the listed price. Royalties read from EIP-2981 and go to the artist on every resale.

04

Treasury

A 2.5% protocol fee on secondary sales, hard-capped at 5% in the contract. Fees are native ETH on Base, paid to the treasury address on settlement.

For artists

Three calls to come onchain.

01

Deploy

Deploy a collection from contracts/art, or point the registry at one you already own.

02

Register

Call register(collection, name, category, metadataURI) from the artist wallet.

03

List

Approve the marketplace for the token, then list(collection, tokenId, price).
