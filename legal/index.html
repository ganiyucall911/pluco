Legal

The fine print, plainly.

A short summary of what PLUCO is, what it charges and what it does not do.

Terms

Five points. No longer than it needs to be.

01

Software, as is

PLUCO is software. The contracts and this interface are provided as is, without warranty. You are responsible for the wallets and transactions you sign.

02

Fees

A 2.5% protocol fee applies to secondary sales and is hard-capped at 5% in the contract. Minting and listing carry no PLUCO fee. Network gas is paid to Base, not to PLUCO.

03

No custody

PLUCO never holds your tokens or your funds. Sellers keep the token until a sale settles, and payments route directly to the seller, the artist and the treasury.

04

Content

Artists are responsible for the work they register and for the rights to it. The registry is permissionless; it does not screen or endorse what is listed.

05

Contact

Questions, bugs and take-down requests go through the project repository on GitHub.
