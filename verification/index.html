Trust

Verification, spelled out.

The badge means one thing: the name attached to it controls the contract behind the work, and the claims on the page match the chain. Nothing more is implied.

PLUCOVerified creatorVerified creator

Example of the mark in use. It appears next to the creator name wherever that creator is credited — never on its own, never on a page that merely mentions the name.

Requirements

  1. 01Registered nameA single creator name, used on every listing and mint. No aliases, no studio suffixes.
  2. 02Onchain historyAt least one collection or minted piece on Base, with the contract address disclosed.
  3. 03Proven controlThe registering wallet must sign from the address that holds the contract or mint.
  4. 04Truthful metadataSupply, edition size and the artwork description must match what is onchain.
  5. 05No misleading claimsNo unregistered copyright, no claimed affiliation with brands or athletes, no promises of returns.
  6. 06ContactableA working contact address that answers within seven days.

What the badge does not mean.

It is not an endorsement of price, rarity or investment value. It does not protect a buyer from a seller changing a listing, and it is not a guarantee that a collection will mint. Verification can be withdrawn if a claim stops being true.

How to be verified.

Register the collection, then send the artist name, the contract address and a signature from the controlling wallet. Review is manual and takes one to two days.
